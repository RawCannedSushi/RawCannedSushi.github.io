YouTube Embed HTML Tutorial

A walkthrough on how to embed a YouTube video into your website or project.


Tutorial

Step 1: Find your video

The first step is to find the video you are wanting to embed. After finding the video, navigate to its page and click the share button. After clicking the share button, a menu should pop up with multiple share options.

In the share menu you should see an option for embed, click that and copy all of the code that appears.


Step 2: Paste the code

After copying the code, navigate to your HTML file and paste the code where you want the video to appear on your wepage.


Step 3: Editing your embed

In order to edit the size of your embed, you can either directly edit the width and height of the iframe, or do the reccomended method of putting the embed within a div and modifying that instead.
